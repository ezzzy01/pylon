What it does

One assistant for sales, stock, money and management.

Pylon AI works through your B1 every night and is ready for questions all day.

Ask anything, in plain words

“Who owes us the most?” “Which items do I need to reorder?” “Show monthly sales as a chart.” Pylon looks it up live in B1 and answers with the table, chart or document you need. Names in the answer open the customer, item or document.

Questions

Good to know.

Does Pylon AI change our B1 data?

No. It reads B1 through a read-only SQL login. If you switch on B1 drafts, it can create a draft document only after a person replies yes to its preview; someone still opens the draft in B1, checks it and adds it.

Does our data leave our building?

B1 data, the analysis and the AI model all stay on your server; your data is not sent to an outside AI service. The optional market watch and web search only look up public information on the internet.

What server do we need?

A modern PC or server is enough to start; the reference setup is a 24-thread CPU with 32 GB of memory. We size it with you for the number of companies and people.

Which SAP Business One versions are supported?

SAP Business One 10.0 on Microsoft SQL Server. Ask us about other versions.

Can it answer in our language and use our terms?

Yes. Each company has its own instructions for tone, language, focus and house terms.

Can we trust the numbers?

Figures use B1's own definitions (for example gross profit as B1 calculates it) so they can be checked line by line against B1 reports. Every answer shows where it came from and how fresh the data is.

Can we try it before we decide?

Yes. Ask the demo a question right away, or open the full app as a guest for 24 hours. Both use SAP's sample company, not real customer data.
